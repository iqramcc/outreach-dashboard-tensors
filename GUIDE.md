# Using the dashboard

For everyone on the outreach team. Ask an admin for your login.

---

## Finding your schools

The **Sheets** page has two tabs — the same schools, sorted two ways:

- **By district & region** — Primary Target and Secondary, split by district
- **By assigned person** — what each volunteer is working on

Two icons appear there and they behave differently:

| | |
|---|---|
| 📄 **Sheet** | A real list. You can add rows and drag them into order. |
| ▤ **View** | Assembled from a filter. Always up to date, but no adding or reordering — open the sheet underneath for that. |

A school is never in two places at once. A view just shows it from a different angle.

---

## Working a sheet

**Click any cell to edit it.** Enter saves, Escape cancels. Dropdowns save on the spot.

**Search** and **Filters** are separate. Search is the box — press Enter. Filters is the button; pick what you want and press **Filter**. Nothing is applied until you do.

The **Status** column is the colour key at the top of the sheet. Changing it also records who changed it and when, so the history builds itself.

**Reordering rows:** drag a row by its number, or type a number into it to send it there. Tick several first and they move together.

---

## Logging a call

Open a school (the **View** link at the end of its row) and use **Log an attempt**.

Do the whole thing in one go — channel, what happened, and **Next follow-up**. That date is what puts the school in the *Follow-ups due* list on the dashboard, so a call logged without one is a call you'll forget to chase.

Every school also has a **Numbers** panel. Add as many as you like — office, principal, POC — each with a name.

---

## Mail and WhatsApp lists

Tick some rows, then **Add to list…**.

A dialog opens showing every school you picked with everything the admin will receive, already filled in from the sheet.

All of it is editable — so if you spot a wrong email while you're there, fix it. Anything you change is **saved onto the school**, not just onto this list.

Fields marked **\*** are compulsory. You can't add until all of them are filled, and there's a tick box to show only the schools still needing attention.

There's also a **Comment** box on each row — optional, for anything the person sending should know ("call the office first"). It stays with that list and doesn't touch the school.

> **Comment** and **Comments** can't be used as column names, so the two don't get confused. For a note that belongs to the school itself, use **Remarks**.

## Making it yours

Two things are yours alone and invisible to everyone else:

**My colours** (button above the sheet)
- Recolour any status just for you — handy for giving two statuses the same colour when they mean the same thing to you
- Invent your own marks ("call after exams") in any colour, then tick rows and apply one from the selection bar

**Your column layout** (the **Columns** button)
- Show or hide columns, and drag a heading in the table to reorder
- *Reset to default* puts it back

---

## Shared vs yours alone

Worth knowing before you change something:

| Yours alone | Everyone sees it |
|---|---|
| My colours and marks | Any cell you edit |
| Which columns you see, and their order | Adding or renaming a **column** |
| | Creating or renaming a **sheet** |
| | Status, assignments, call logs |

Adding a column adds it to **every** school, not just the one you're looking at.

---

## Things you can't do

These are admin-only: importing Excel, changing the shared colour key, changing whether a school is Primary or Secondary, managing the team, and deleting sheets.

If you need one of them, ask an admin — don't work around it.
